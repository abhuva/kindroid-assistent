# Kindroid Lab Notes

**A working notebook about understanding conversational archives.**

This site records the process of building a local archive tool: the questions that
shape it, the experiments that change it, and the limits of what its results can
tell us. It combines a dated journal with reference pages for methods that evolve
over time.

The focus is exploratory. Embeddings can reveal similar passages; explicit
questions can produce a different view of the same material. Neither makes a
cluster a diagnosis, a score a measurement of a person's inner state, or an
association a causal relationship.
